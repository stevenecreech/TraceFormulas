**Result ID:** `linear-algebra.trace-cyclic`.

For nonnegative integers $m,n$, an $m\times n$ complex matrix $A$, and an $n\times m$ complex matrix $B$,

$$
\operatorname{tr}(AB)=\operatorname{tr}(BA).
$$

The proof expands matrix multiplication, interchanges finite sums, and commutes complex scalar factors. It includes zero-sized matrices and does not require either matrix to be invertible.
